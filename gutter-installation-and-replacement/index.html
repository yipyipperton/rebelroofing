LET’S UNDERSTAND YOUR ROOF

The roof is only part of the picture.

Gutters and downspouts help direct roof runoff away from the exterior of your home. When they overflow, sag, or drain poorly, it is worth looking at the whole route the water takes. Rebel can discuss gutter installation, replacement, and coordination with a roofing project.

01

A fit for your home

Ask about seamless gutter options, dimensions, finishes, and downspout placement for the roof and exterior.

02

Drainage that makes sense

Discuss pitch, outlets, discharge locations, and the concerns you’ve noticed during rainfall.

03

Repair or replacement

Cracks, separation, sagging, and recurring overflow can have different causes. An inspection helps define the scope.

04

Coordinate with your roof

If you are replacing your roof, discuss the gutter condition at the same time and whether coordinated work makes sense.

Do I need new gutters with a new roof?

Not necessarily. Their condition and how the roofing work affects them should guide the decision. Ask for the gutter scope separately.

How much does gutter replacement cost?

Cost depends on measurements, material, access, downspouts, and the work required. Request a quote for your property.

What warning signs should I look for?

Overflow, sagging, staining below gutters, separation, and water pooling near the home are useful concerns to mention.
