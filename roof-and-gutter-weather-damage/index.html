LET’S UNDERSTAND YOUR ROOF

A roof assessment. A clearer way forward.

Storm damage is not always obvious from the ground. Tell Rebel Roofing what you noticed and when it happened. The inspection can help establish the condition of the roof and gutters and the work that may be needed. Ask what photographs and written documentation are available for your records.

01

Start with an inspection

Discuss visible concerns, affected roof areas, and gutters. Do not assume every storm calls for a complete replacement.

02

Understand the scope

Ask which issues appear related to the storm and what repair or replacement options are proposed.

03

Keep the roles clear

Rebel can discuss roofing work and supporting documentation. Your insurance company determines coverage and claim decisions.

04

Plan the restoration

Review scope, materials, payment responsibilities, and scheduling before authorizing the work.
