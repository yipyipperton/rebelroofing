LET’S UNDERSTAND YOUR ROOF

Choose a system, not just a finish.

A metal roof changes the look of a property and brings a different set of installation considerations. Panel profile, fastening, flashing, ventilation, and product specifications all matter. An inspection and project discussion can help narrow the choices for your home or building.

01

Profile & appearance

Discuss panel profiles, finishes, and available colors. Look at physical samples alongside the exterior of your property.

02

System details

Ask which system is proposed and how transitions, penetrations, and fastening will be addressed.

03

Idaho conditions

Discuss snow, drainage, exposure, and any roof-specific requirements. Performance varies by product and installation.

04

Maintenance & warranty

Review inspection needs, care instructions, exclusions, and the product and workmanship terms for the proposed system.

Is every metal roof the same?

No. Materials, profiles, finishes, fastening methods, and installation details vary. Compare the specific system proposed for your property.

Is a metal roof maintenance-free?

No roof should be assumed maintenance-free. Follow the product and installation guidance and ask about regular inspections.

Can you explain the cost difference?

Request a project-specific comparison that includes preparation, materials, installation scope, and applicable warranties.
