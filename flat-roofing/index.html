LET’S UNDERSTAND YOUR ROOF

Start with the roof. Consider the operation.

Low-slope roofing needs a property-specific assessment. Existing materials, drainage, penetrations, rooftop equipment, and access can all shape a project. Talk through the building’s requirements and what needs to stay accessible while roofing work is underway.

01

System assessment

Identify the existing roof and discuss repair or replacement options for the conditions found.

02

TPO, PVC & EPDM

Compare the proposed material, attachment, compatibility, and details. Product suitability depends on the project.

03

Access & coordination

Discuss staging, tenant communication, equipment access, and working hours before agreeing to the schedule.

04

Scope & documentation

Review the preparation, roof details, drainage considerations, exclusions, and warranty terms in the proposal.
