Ask About Financing

Look beyond the
monthly payment.

Start with an inspection and a project quote. Then compare the financing options available for that scope of work.

  1. Amount financedUnderstand the project price, any down payment, and the total borrowed.
  2. APR and feesAsk for the annual percentage rate, fees, and any promotional conditions.
  3. Payment scheduleCompare the monthly payment, number of payments, and total repayment.
  4. Approval and termsReview eligibility and the lender’s actual documents before accepting.

Is zero-down financing available?

Ask about current options for qualified customers. Approval and terms depend on the lender, applicant, and project.

Can you quote a monthly payment here?

A meaningful payment example needs the amount financed, APR, repayment term, and lender conditions. Contact the team to discuss current plans for your project.

Does requesting an inspection mean I am applying for credit?

No. Requesting an inspection is not a credit application. Discuss any financing application directly with the team and the lender.
