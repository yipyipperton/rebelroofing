MAKING THE DECISION

Repair or replace? Start with the roof you have.

A leak tells you something needs attention. It does not tell you the full scope.

Get a Free Inspection
01

Look at the extent

A localized problem and widespread deterioration call for different discussions. Ask how much of the roof is affected and whether adjacent areas are sound.

02

Consider its history

Tell the inspector about previous repairs, recurring leaks, and the roof’s approximate age if known. That context helps explain the concern.

03

Compare what each option addresses

Ask what a repair would fix, what it would leave unchanged, and when replacement may be worth considering. Compare specific scopes rather than headline prices.

04

Make the decision with an inspection

General advice cannot determine what a specific property needs. Start with an assessment and a project-specific proposal.
