LET’S UNDERSTAND YOUR ROOF

A new roof starts with the right questions.

Recurring leaks, worn shingles, or widespread damage may make replacement worth considering. An inspection helps determine whether a repair can address the problem or whether a new roof is the more practical option. We’ll talk through the condition, your material preferences, and the scope that makes sense for your home.

01

Know what’s included

Ask about tear-off, disposal, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and the assessment of the decking beneath the shingles.

02

Choose the look

Compare shingle profiles and colors against your siding, trim, and surroundings. Review actual samples before making a selection.

03

Understand the schedule

Roof size, condition, weather, and material availability all affect timing. Some replacements can take one day; confirm the plan for yours.

04

Review the fine print

Discuss how hidden damage, additional work, payment, and product or workmanship warranties will be handled.

Does an older roof always need replacing?

No. Age is one factor. The condition of the materials, extent of damage, and repair history also matter. An inspection helps establish the options.

Can I switch from asphalt to metal?

Ask about a metal roofing assessment. Roof design, system requirements, appearance, and budget all influence whether it is a suitable option.

What happens if damaged decking is discovered?

Ask how concealed damage would be documented, priced, and approved before signing the proposal.
